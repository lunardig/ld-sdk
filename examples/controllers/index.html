Sample controllers for Lunar Dig

Each page below is an ordinary web page that watches and runs a Lunar Dig claim from its own window, through the claim host protocol (ldcp-0). The game opens them; the player approves what each may do; every command they send is recorded in the claim's history under the page's address.

  • Spreadsheet: live stock and job rates; pause and resume jobs (read, operate)
  • Schematic: machines and connections; disconnect a connection (read, build)
  • Operator: a script that pauses jobs that stall for want of power, and resumes them when power returns (read, operate)

Source: lunardig/ld-sdk, examples/controllers. Guide: ld-sdk guide §8.
